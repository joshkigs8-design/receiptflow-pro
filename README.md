# Receiptflow Pro

Rent Receipt Pro — Premium AI-Powered Property Management & Digital Receipt Platform

Project Overview

Build a premium, production-ready SaaS web application called Rent Receipt Pro.

This platform enables landlords, property managers, apartment owners, hostels, and real estate companies to manage rental properties, tenants, rent payments, and automatically generate professional digital rent receipts.

The platform must also include a secure Tenant Portal where tenants can log in or verify their identity to view, print, and download their receipts online.

The overall design should rival premium SaaS products such as Stripe, Airbnb, Linear, Notion, and Vercel.
